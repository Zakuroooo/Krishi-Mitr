What the walkthrough covers

1

Today's real price

The mandi rate minus transport, hamali and commission — what actually reaches his hand, not the number on the board.

2

The verdict, with both numbers

Wait or sell, with the expected gain and the worst case shown at the same size. When the band is too wide, it refuses to advise.

3

Liquidity to act on it

Deposit at a partner warehouse and draw cash against the stored lot, so waiting becomes affordable.

4

A direct, verified sale

Verified buyers, escrow-backed payment and a dispute window before release — the whole loop, spoken in Marathi.
